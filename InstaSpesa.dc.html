GROCERY E-COMMERCE · UX REDESIGN

Istant Spesa

Redesigning a supermarket’s online store around the pain points that made me abandon the real one.

TYPE

University final project

COURSE

Advanced Human-Computer Interaction

FOCUS

User research · IA · UI

THE PRODUCT

An online grocery store: browse aisles, build a cart, check out — without the friction.

WHY

I’d worked in a supermarket, then abandoned its e-commerce — too hard to navigate. That became the brief.

MAIN CHALLENGES

  • Findability — locating one item in a huge catalogue.
  • Information architecture — hundreds of categories, always knowing where you are.
  • Cart clarity — total, quantities and substitutions in plain sight.
